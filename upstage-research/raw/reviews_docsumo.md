The core engine handles document indexing, classification, extraction, validation, and automated decision-making through custom AI models trained with minimal sample documents.\n\nSmart table extraction pulls structured data from unstructured documents—critical for processing complex financial statements or claims forms. The platform includes document pre-processing functionality and API integration architecture for embedding within existing enterprise systems.\n\nIndustry-specific solutions target [financial services](https://docsumo.com/solutions) (invoice processing), real estate (lease agreement automation), insurance (ACORD form processing), logistics (bill of lading analysis), and healthcare/utilities (utility bill management). The API-first architecture differentiates from standalone tools, allowing organizations to embed document processing within broader automation workflows rather than forcing users into a separate platform.\n\n![Docsumo website screenshot](https://checkthat.ai/cdn-cgi/image/width=640,format=auto,fit=scale-down,quality=80/https://checkthat-media-files.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/company-screenshots/docsumo-screenshot-1763283773281.png)\n\nView Full Page\n\n## Docsumo Target Market\n\n[Documented customers](https://docsumo.com/customers) reveal Docsumo serves mid-market to enterprise organizations with accuracy-critical financial workflows.
